💧 Our Story

About AquaBank Rwanda

We are building Kigali’s water resilience infrastructure — capturing seasonal abundance and delivering safe, tested water when communities need it most.

Mission

Why AquaBank Exists

Kigali receives significant seasonal rainfall, yet dry-season water shortages affect households and businesses across the city. Millions of litres of rainwater run off unused every wet season while communities struggle during dry periods.

AquaBank Rwanda was created to bridge this gap — capturing water when it is abundant, storing it safely, treating and testing it to the required standard, and distributing it reliably when demand is highest.

We believe water security is not a luxury. It is a foundation for health, dignity and economic growth.

Our Mission

“To capture, store, treat and distribute safe water to Kigali households and businesses — reliably, affordably and sustainably.”

Our Vision

“A water-resilient Rwanda where communities and businesses can access reliable water throughout the year.”

Our Tagline

Store Now, Never Run Dry.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

We publish our water quality results, pricing logic and operational data. Customers deserve to know what they are drinking.
