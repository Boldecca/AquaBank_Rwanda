FAQ

How does AquaBank Rwanda work?

We capture water during wetter periods, store it safely, treat it, test it, and distribute it through kiosks or delivery channels when demand rises.

Who can use the service?

Households, salons, restaurants, laundromats, schools, apartments and businesses can all benefit from AquaBank service depending on their water needs.

Do you offer subscriptions?

Yes. Household and recurring service plans are designed to make access more predictable and easier to manage.

What kind of delivery options do you offer?

Customers can access pre-paid kiosk pickup, scheduled delivery, or smart-metered service depending on the solution selected.

Is the water tested?

Water quality testing is an important part of the AquaBank process. Treatment and quality checks are performed before distribution.

Can businesses request a custom service plan?

Yes. Businesses, schools and other institutions can enquire about custom volume and service arrangements.
