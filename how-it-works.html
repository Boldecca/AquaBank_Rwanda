How it works

From wet-season capture to reliable daily supply.

AquaBank Rwanda follows a simple flow: capture rainfall and surplus water, store it safely, treat it, test it, and deliver it where it is needed most.

☁️
Water Bank
Flow

The AquaBank system

1
🌧️

Capture

Rainwater and surplus water are collected from rooftops, channels and approved intake points.

2
🛢️

Store

Water is moved into sealed storage tanks that help buffer seasonal demand.

3
🧪

Filter

Pre-treatment supports the removal of solids and improves water quality before distribution.

4
🧴

Disinfect

Disinfection and monitoring help reduce risk and support safer drinking water.

5
✅

Test

Routine quality checks cover pH, turbidity and chlorine-related monitoring requirements.

6
🚚

Distribute

Water reaches homes, businesses and institutions through kiosks, scheduled delivery and smart metering.

Customer flow

How customers get water

📱

Prepaid kiosk

Customers top up water credits and collect water at a trusted local kiosk.

🚐

Scheduled delivery

Orders are planned to suit households, salons, restaurants and service businesses.

📡

Smart-metered connection

Usage is tracked and managed for more structured, dependable service.

Operational model

Simple service, strong quality control.

From collection and storage to testing and delivery, each stage is designed to keep the product safe, traceable and easy for customers to use.

  • Monitoring of storage conditions and tank levels
  • Routine quality checks for visible and measurable water quality indicators
  • Clear route planning for scheduled delivery and pickup
  • Customer support and account tracking for repeat buyers
